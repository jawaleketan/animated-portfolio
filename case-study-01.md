# CASE STUDY: Driving Authority and AI Visibility for a B2B SaaS Platform

## Executive Summary

This case study outlines the organic growth strategy for **Work 365** (work365apps.com), a leading subscription management and billing automation solution for Microsoft Partners. By focusing on high-intent keyword acquisition and modernizing the site for AI-driven search, we achieved significant gains in keyword footprint and established a strong presence in AI-generated search results.

---

## 📊 Performance Highlights (As of Jan 2026)

* **Total Organic Keywords:** 798 (+8.13% growth)
* **Domain Authority Score:** 27 (Steady growth of +1)
* **AI Visibility Score:** 14 (Outperforming competitors in ChatGPT and Gemini citations)
* **Referring Domains:** 484 with 5.6K high-quality backlinks.

---

## 🛡️ The Challenge

The B2B SaaS space—specifically the Microsoft Partner ecosystem—is highly competitive. The primary challenges included:

1. **Niche Competition:** Competing against established legacy billing systems.
2. **Algorithm Volatility:** Maintaining traffic stability during Google’s transition toward AI-centric search.
3. **Authority Building:** Increasing the Domain Authority (AS) to compete for high-difficulty "Money Keywords."

---

## 🛠️ The Strategy

### 1. Semantic Content & Keyword Expansion

Instead of targeting high-volume generic terms, we focused on **Long-Tail Intent**.

* **Result:** Increased organic keywords to **798**, a growth of **8.13%** in the recent period.
* **Tactic:** Developed a "Hub and Spoke" content model around billing automation and Microsoft CSP integration.

### 2. Optimization for AI Search (GEO)

With the rise of AI Overviews (SGE) and LLMs, we optimized content for Generative Engine Optimization (GEO).

* **Result:** Secured **32 cited pages** across ChatGPT, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews.
* **Tactic:** Implemented structured data (Schema.org), used clear Q&A formatting, and focused on "Natural Language" responses to common industry pain points.

### 3. Technical SEO & Authority Building

* **Link Profile:** Maintained a healthy backlink profile of **5.6K links** from **484 referring domains**.
* **Tactic:** Conducted a backlink audit to prune low-quality spam and focused on guest posting on high-authority tech and cloud-service publications.
